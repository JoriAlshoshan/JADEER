# JADEER – AI-Powered Business Validation Platform

**JADEER** is an AI-powered platform that helps entrepreneurs evaluate and improve their business ideas through intelligent analysis and strategic recommendations.

Inspired by my graduation project, **Jadwa AI**, JADEER was independently developed from scratch in just **two days** during the **Kanz AI Training Hackathon (July 2026)** using Replit Agent and the OpenAI API.

## Key Features

- **Business Validation:** AI-generated Venture Scores and investment readiness assessments.
- **Score Breakdown:** Evaluation across six dimensions: Venture, Innovation, Market, Risk, Revenue, and Execution.
- **Business Analysis:** SWOT analysis, business models, market opportunities, and customer personas.
- **Risk & Competition:** Risk assessments, mitigation strategies, and competitive landscape analysis.
- **Strategic Planning:** Revenue models, go-to-market strategies, and 90-day execution roadmaps.
- **Improve Strategy:** Generates actionable recommendations to strengthen business ideas and address identified weaknesses.
- **Dashboard & Reports:** Project management, bilingual support (Arabic/English), and PDF export.

## Development & AI Integration

JADEER was built using **Replit Agent** for AI-assisted development and integrated with the **OpenAI API** to generate business evaluations and improvement strategies.

Unlike Jadwa AI, which combines Machine Learning and Generative AI, JADEER uses **LLM-based analysis** without a separately trained ML model.

**Technologies:** Replit, OpenAI API, Generative AI, Agentic AI, Prompt Engineering, React, TypeScript, Node.js.
